# Personas

## P1: Competitive Strategist
- Wants deterministic rules and minimal ambiguity.
- Cares about fairness and replayability.

## P2: Casual Group Player
- Wants low friction setup and readable UI.
- Cares about pace and clarity.

## P3: Content Creator (Post-MVP)
- Wants map packs and scenario flexibility.
- Cares about validation and tooling.
